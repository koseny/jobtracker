# Product Definition — CivilBonus

**Baseline:** v0.1  
**Status:** PROPOSED

## Purpose

CivilBonus is being developed as a controlled web application/product. The detailed product proposition, target users, business model and MVP boundary are intentionally not invented in this baseline; they must be defined and approved before feature expansion.

## Current proven capability

A visitor can authenticate with a Google account through Firebase Authentication, see authenticated identity information, and sign out.

## Product governance

- Product scope is defined before implementation.
- New structural conventions require explicit approval.
- Experiments may validate uncertainty, but successful experiments are documented before becoming architectural baseline.
- Features must have acceptance criteria before implementation.
- Scope not explicitly approved remains out of baseline.

## Next product-definition work

Define and approve:
1. target user/persona;
2. problem/job-to-be-done;
3. value proposition;
4. MVP outcomes and exclusions;
5. primary user journeys;
6. business/monetization assumptions;
7. success measures.
